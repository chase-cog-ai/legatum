## 2026-11-25: A winning AI's Peace price rarely bites, because a winning AI seldom wants Peace

- Cause: `diplomacy.rs::wants_peace_why` ends, for an AI with no routed plan, in the gate "no plan: age and strength": Peace only while our army is under 130% of the enemy's (or we are in danger, or leaving a coalition's war). The deals "winning" line is 150%, so a no-plan AI that is winning already refuses every Peace, paid or not; the price (refuse under half of 25% of the loser's treasury, counter with an ask) only changes play for a Hard AI whose plan fights elsewhere ("plan fights elsewhere" wants Peace at any ratio).
- Evidence: `crates/ai/tests/deals.rs` exploration on the three-empire Sparta situation: at 150%, 200% and 300% the AI neither offered nor accepted a free Peace, with or without deals; the winning rules are tested at the scoring level (`deals.rs::tests`).
- What changed: nothing beyond the docs. If the designer wants a winning AI to sell Peace (accept a truce that pays its price even when it would refuse a free one), that needs a Gold term inside `wants_peace_why`'s strength gate, not in the answer to the offer.
- Also: an integration test of anything that reads other empires' cities must reveal them (`visibility::reveal_all_to_team`); `blank_game` cities beyond sight are not in `PlayerView::cities`, so "between" or "neighbour" rules silently read nothing.
