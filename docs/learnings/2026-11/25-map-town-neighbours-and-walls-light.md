## 2026-11-25: Peaks and ruins beside towns step back; Walls finishing gets a light, not a sketch-in

- **Crowding (PR 129 UI review follow-up).** The town is drawn over the organic land art in the terrain cache, so a peak, hill or wood on the hex in front of or beside a town never covers it, but it still runs into it (Zürich's star fort sat on a peak's tip). Ruins are drawn in screen space after the cache, so they did cover the town (Cologne's east bastion). Both now go through `cityArt.ts::clearOfTown`: the town's footprint is an oval (`TOWN_OVAL`, from `cityArtFootprint`), the neighbour's painted mass is an oval measured from its sprite's alpha box (`ORGANIC_MASS`, `RUIN_MASS`), and the neighbour steps straight away from the town (up to 0.4 hex radius) and shrinks (to 70-72%), taking the least change that clears. Art behind the town (more than 0.75 radius above it) is left alone: the town covers it, which reads as depth. A scattered cluster that still can't clear is dropped (`keepClearOfTowns`); a tile's centred cluster always stays.
- **Gotcha.** The ruin is drawn `r = 0.88 * hexPx` either side of its centre, so `r` is half the painting's side, not its side: the first version passed 0.44 of it as the mass and nothing moved. Check a keep-clear change against a before/after shot, not only the unit test.
- **Gotcha.** A clear test with no room to move (`maxPush: 0`, `minScale: 1`) falls through to the limits and returns the input unchanged, so it can't be used as an "is it clear?" check; test the oval directly.
- **Gotcha (UI review).** Clearing the oval by 1.5 world units left Cologne's ruins and Zürich's peak still touching: wall towers and bastions stand past the ground oval, and 1.5 units is about 1.5 px. `TOWN_GAP` is 5 units (about 5 px at z1.1). Likewise near-white light screened over pale stone washes the masonry out flat, and a saturated gilt in soft light turns it garish yellow: the payoff is `#ffe0a0` in `soft-light` (band alphas 0.5/0.7/0.9), 1.3x stronger below z1. A peak's tip rises well above an oval fitted to its alpha box's middle, so `ORGANIC_MASS.Mountains` uses the box's full half height.
- **Walls payoff.** The UI review proposed sketching the ring in (ink, then wash). That is the reveal the designer rejected for construction ("outlined, then filled in", 2026-10-24), and construction animation is off behind `CONSTRUCTION_ANIMATION`. The payoff is instead the promotion glint's vocabulary: one soft band of warm light over the ring's stones and clipped out where the town stands in front of the back wall (`litRing`), left to right in 1.5 s, after the moves. It fires from the view diff (Walls level up in a city seen before and after, same owner), so a capture or a city coming out of the fog does not trigger it, and it plays only with Map animations on and no reduced motion, like every moment.
