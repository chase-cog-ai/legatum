# Forts stop a unit once, on entering their reach (2026-11-25)

Forts (rules 63, `rules::forts`) block the road: a unit that steps next to a Fort held by a military unit it is at War with stops for the turn. The first version stopped every step that ended next to the Fort, which froze a unit beside it for as many turns as it had adjacent tiles to cross (a unit walking round a Fort lost three turns, and a unit that began its turn beside one could never leave at more than one tile a turn). The rule now tests both ends of the step: it stops only when the step starts outside the Fort's reach (`from` not adjacent) and ends inside it. A Fort therefore costs a passer exactly one turn, a unit already beside it moves freely, and the answer is a pure function of the step and the board, so it is the same in `movement_round`, the reach overlay and the route's turn numbers (`app/src/forts.ts`).

The stop is applied where the step lands in `movement_round`, after conflicts resolve, so it never changes who wins a tile; it only zeroes the mover's remaining moves and keeps its destination.
