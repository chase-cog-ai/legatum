## 2026-11-25: A natural wonder's "+25%" belongs in the tile's yield icons when they show

- **Change.** With the Yields overlay's icons on, a wonder tile carried four marks (painting, yield cluster, "+25%" pill, medallion), and the pill needed a slot hunt around banners, towns and unit counters (`wonderBadgeAt`). The bonus is now part of the cluster: `LensTile.bonus` names the boosted yield (`overlays.ts`), and `yieldCluster` puts a small parchment "+25%" pill right after that yield's icons. `foldedBonuses` decides per tile; `drawWonderBadges` skips the pill for folded tiles, and `render.ts` strips `bonus` from the rest before drawing the icons.
- **Gotcha.** A tile can have none of the boosted yield (Giant's Causeway on Hills: no Production). Appending a lone icon there would read as 1 Production, since the cluster's rule is one icon per point. The tag then carries the icon after the text, as the pill did ("+25% [icon]"), and the icon is not a point.
- **Gotcha.** The first version set "+25%" in the yield's ink at 0.78 of the icon size: about 6 px at a normal zoom, unreadable on dark wonder paintings, and drawn under a unit's counter (the pill had stepped clear of counters or drawn above them), and its widened cluster ran into a neighbour's medallion. A folded mark must keep the old mark's guarantees. The pill now has a fixed 10 px font; `fittedCluster` shrinks only the icons (to 0.6 at most) to fit the hex, and returns nothing when it can't, and a unit counter on the cluster also keeps the pill. At normal zoom most wonder tiles therefore keep the pill; the fold shows close in.
