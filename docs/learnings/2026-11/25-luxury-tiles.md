# Luxury tiles: a third resource class without a new system (2026-11-25)

- Luxuries are `Resource` values with `ResourceClass::Luxury`, so the map view, memory, medallions, tile card and the city's "Resources: …" line carried them with no new field. What differs from a bonus resource is one fact: `improvements::kind_for` returns `None`, and `tile_resource_yields` pays the bonus unimproved.
- Placement is its own derived stream (`LAYER_LUXURIES`) run after `wonders::place`, so a seed keeps its terrain, starts, deposits and natural wonders; only six tiles gain a resource. It is still a rules bump (60): tapes regenerate the map from the seed and yields differ.
- `kind_for` returning an `Option` is the compile-time check that every caller decides what a resource with no improvement means; the app mirrors it (`improvementKindFor` is `ImprovementKind | null` from `rules.generated.json`).
- A third category colour has to pass `lensPalette.test.ts` (10 CIEDE2000 from bonus green and strategic red for every kind of colour sight); gold `#d1a21f` does.
- New resource medallions fail `tools/art/distinctness.py` in greyscale when they are a mid-tone subject on a mid-tone field (the first Silk, Spices, Incense and Furs all sat on Copper, Iron and Cattle at 14 px). Colour does not count: the check takes the worst of normal, colour-blind and greyscale sight, and needs a mean gap of 16 Delta E over the whole disc. Give each new medallion its own light-dark layout (top to bottom, left and right, dark over light) in the prompt; `--set resources --top 200` reruns in about five seconds.
