# 2026-11-25: The rivals table replaced the race panel's fold

- **What:** the right sidebar's "Your standing" block and the "All empires" fold (per-row VP tokens with hover cards, the "Nobody yet" row, the key and the capitals rule) became one always-open rivals table (`app/src/ui/RivalsTable.tsx`), Civ 6 style, at Sam's request. The table shows VP, Peace or War, Gold, Army, Cities and Science per turn; an unmet empire shows only its VP.
- **Science per turn had to become public.** `PlayerPublic` carried gold, army, economy and advancement but not Science per turn; it now has `science` (`economy::player_income(..).science.total`, 0 once dead). `public_view` now runs `player_income` once per player per turn; watch it in the perf supervisor's numbers if turn time moves.
- **Unmet is judged by `view.diplomacy.met`.** A missing `met` (saves from before first contact) means everyone counts as met, as the Diplomacy panel already does. The Score ledger still names every empire; hiding unmet empires there too would be a separate change.
- **Dead code went with the fold:** `useTokenCards`, `TokenCard`, `HeldToken`, `PendingGoal`, `NobodyRow`, `RowWho`, `CAPITALS_RULE` (VpHoldings.tsx) and `SourceCard`, `SourceMedal` and their helpers (vpParts.tsx), with their CSS. The held-goal "click its token to show it on the map" hint went too; the map marks still show where a held goal stands.
