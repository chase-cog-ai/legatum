# Cutting a strategic deposit: balance census and what the rule reuses (2026-11-25)

- The rule (rules 60): an enemy military unit standing on a strategic deposit cuts its supply while it stands there. It is one `Deposit::cut` field read by `Deposit::supply`, so every count (`supplied`, the shortage rule, the breakdown, the view) follows without a second code path. "Enemy" is `rules::is_threat` and "military unit" is `rules::is_military`: at peace a foreign unit cuts nothing (camping on a neighbour's tile was an exploit before), and aircraft never stand on a tile.
- No pillage and no state: the cut is derived from where units stand, so the save shape is unchanged. The report lines come from comparing `resources::cut_deposits` before and after the turn (`announce_cuts`); a deposit lost with its tile is not reported as restored.
- Balance census, `supply_census 256 9700001` (8 Hard empires, Standard map), master against this rule on the same seeds. Conquest is not faster: cities captured a game 22.42 → 22.84, median game length 223 → 225 turns, median cities and land army equal at every snapshot (T50 to T250) but one (13 against 12 land units at T200). Empires with an idle user: 2% → 3% at T100, 8% → 9% at T150, 19% → 23% at T200, 27% → 27% at T250.
- How often it happens with an AI that does not aim for it: 31 cuts a game (3.4 by barbarians), 5.6 of 8 empires cut at least once, a cut takes 2.7 supply and lasts 1.7 turns, and 10 cuts a game leave a user idle. The Hard AI has no rule that seeks a deposit to cut or guards its own; these are units standing where a war took them.
- The rule changes fixed-seed games (the `scripted_hard_city_fire` tape pin failed), so it needs `tools/sim/repin.sh` as well as the version-bump steps.
- Hard duels (`legatum-sim --games 96 --players 2 --difficulty hard`), master against the rule: 75 capitals, 19 VP, 1 conquest and 1 stall against 76 capitals, 19 VP and 1 stall; median 191 turns both; 4.9 against 4.4 cities captured a game. The stall moved from seed 96 to seed 14, which `crates/sim/tests/hold_metrics.rs` played, so that test now plays seeds 17 to 32. The `hard_vs_human` fixed-tape benchmark wins at T161 instead of T170.
